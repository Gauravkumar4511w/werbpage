Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

ArenaCore runs Free Fire tournaments at ffarenacore.vercel.app. This policy explains what we collect when you use the site, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, gamer tag and password, and your mobile number if you add one.
  • Google sign-in: if you continue with Google, we receive your name and verified email address from Google. We do not receive your Google password or anything else from your Google account.
  • Wallet and payments: your coin balances and their history, the UTR or transaction ID you enter for a top-up, and the name of the payment screenshot you upload.
  • Withdrawals: the UPI ID or bank account name, number and IFSC you give us to send your winnings.
  • Matches: the matches you join and the in-game names or IDs you enter for your team, and results such as kills and winnings.
  • Usage: your IP address and a random session ID, used to count players online and keep the service secure.

How we use it

  • To create your account, log you in and verify your email with a one-time code.
  • To run matches: register your team, share room details and pay out prizes.
  • To credit top-ups after we check the UTR, and to send withdrawals to the account you gave us.
  • To prevent fraud, cheating and abuse, and to answer your questions.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who else handles it

  • Vercel hosts the site and its data.
  • Google sends our verification emails through Gmail and provides Google sign-in.
  • Banks and UPI apps receive the details needed to send your withdrawal.

We share data with anyone else only when the law requires it.

Your browser

The site saves your login and some match details in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. Signing out removes your login from that browser.

How long we keep it

We keep your account while it is open. We keep payment and withdrawal records for as long as the law requires, even after an account is closed.

Your choices

You can change your gamer tag, email and mobile number from your profile, and reset your password with Forgot password?. To get a copy of your data or have your account deleted, email us from the address on your account.

Children

ArenaCore is for players aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
